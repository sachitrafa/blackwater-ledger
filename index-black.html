The Blackwater Ledger

Saturday, 17 October, 7:05 AM. Elias Marlowe, 58, restaurateur and one of the richest men on Blackwater Lake, is found dead in the armchair of his study. His housekeeper thought it was his heart.

The coroner isn’t so sure, and the dead man left behind more secrets than answers.

The clock starts now. Every question you ask, every call you make, every thread you pull takes time, and the people in Elias’s life won’t wait for you. Some of them will call you first. Not all of them mean well.

Nobody will connect the dots for you. Narrow the suspects to two. Then make one arrest. You won’t get a second. Turn your sound on.
